About Upskillingz

Why we built this

Our starting question: how can polytechnics encourage students to learn skills outside their own course? Upskillingz is our answer — a small, student-run project to make that first step easier.

The problem

Every polytechnic student's timetable is built around one diploma. But the skills that make you stand out — a bit of data analysis, a design elective, a language, a maritime module — often live outside it, in each school's own elective system.

The information exists. SP, NYP and RP each publish their own elective or module listing every semester. The problem isn't access — it's discovery. Most students never browse it, don't know what's in it, find it late, or don't even know their own school has one.

What we're doing about it

Upskillingz curates those listings into something a student would actually scroll through: tagged by interest, filterable by polytechnic and school, written in plain language instead of course-catalogue speak — then points straight back to the official source to confirm and register.

We're not replacing any school's system. We're the on-ramp to it — a nudge, a Telegram channel, an Instagram post at the right moment, so more students actually take the step. For NP and TP, we don't have a listing to point to yet, so our Telegram channel is where students crowdsource opinions on those electives instead.

What we believe

Three ideas behind the project

  • 1

    Skills compound outside your lane

    The most useful thing you learn in poly might not be on your own course outline.

  • 2

    Discovery is the real barrier

    Students don't skip electives because they don't care — they skip them because they didn't know, or didn't look in time.

  • 3

    Peer-to-peer beats top-down

    A recommendation from another student in a Telegram chat travels further than a line in a handbook.
